Trans Mutual Aid Manchester

About

We are a local mutual aid network in Greater Manchester. Our goal is to ensure that all trans+ people in the city have everything they need for a decent life. Many in our community are struggling for the bare minimum, we hope that one day this will change and there won't be a need for us to exist. Unfortunately poverty, our primary focus, is unlikely to end any time soon.

Get Involved

TMAM is run entirely by volunteers from our community, as with all voluntary organisations our capacity varies depending on how much free time our volunteers have. As such we are always looking for more people to get involved.
